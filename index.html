Booklet Maker

Turn a liturgy PDF into pages you can print, fold, and staple.

Download print-ready booklet

What your PDF should look like

  1. Landscape (sideways) pages.
  2. Two columns per page. Each column becomes one booklet page.
  3. In reading order: the left column of page 1 is the front cover.
  4. An even number of pages.

How to print

  1. Open the downloaded file. Print settings: landscape, 100% / actual size (not “fit to page”).
  2. If your printer prints double-sided, choose flip on short edge and print everything. You’re done — skip to folding.
  3. Otherwise, print odd pages only (1, 3, 5…).
  4. Take the stack out. Without flipping it over, turn it end-over-end (rotate 180°, so the top edge is now at the bottom) and put it back in the tray.
  5. Print even pages only (2, 4, 6…).

Fold and staple

  1. Keep the sheets in the order they came out.
  2. Fold the whole stack in half, then staple on the fold.

Tip: print one copy first to check the pages come out in order. Your file never leaves your computer — all the work happens in your browser.
